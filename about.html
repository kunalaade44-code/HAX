01

My story

I started working with Sheryians Coding School in 2021, while I was still pursuing my engineering degree. What started with software development and teaching gradually turned into something much bigger.

Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to work on almost every side of a technology education business.

I’ve built software products, taught 50,000+ students, created technical content, led educational programs, worked with cross-functional teams, and contributed to growing Sheryians’ offline revenue by 2.5X.

On the technology side, I’ve worked on Sheryians’ course-selling platform and Learning Management System, connecting product development, frontend engineering, UI/UX, and the needs of real students.

As an educator and creator, I’ve helped make software development more accessible to thousands of learners. My work includes technical courses, educational videos, workshops, university talks, and collaborations with people working across the technology industry.

I’ve also been closely involved in the business and operational side of education, managing teams, working with sales and marketing, improving student experience, and helping educational programs grow.

What I enjoy most is the intersection of all these things. I like understanding how products are built, why people use them, how businesses grow, and how good communication can make technology more accessible.

I’m particularly interested in Product Management, EdTech Leadership, Developer Education, Technical Content, Developer Relations, and building products that solve meaningful problems.

I believe my biggest strength is being able to understand a problem from multiple perspectives: as an engineer, as an educator, as someone building the product, and as someone working directly with its users.

Today, I’m continuing to build my independent professional identity while exploring opportunities to work with ambitious people and organizations across technology and education.
